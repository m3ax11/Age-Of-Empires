# National Focus Structure Pass

This file records the required pre-edit structural report for each country. It is deliberately separate from `FOCUS_EDITORIAL_REWORK.md`: titles and prose are judged there, while this pass deals with player choices, prerequisite logic, reconvergence, branch pacing, and capstones.

The machine-readable baseline is generated by `work/focus_structure_audit.py` into `work/audit-results/focus-structure/`. In that report, multiple `focus =` entries inside one `prerequisite` block are treated as OR; multiple separate `prerequisite` blocks are treated as AND. Heuristic endpoint and cross-branch classifications are leads for manual review, not automatic defects.

## Baseline scope

- 39 AOE focus-tree files audited.
- 3,780 focus definitions mapped.
- Existing focus IDs, rewards, icons, country selectors, and scripted effects are preserved unless a recorded structural defect requires a narrow change.
- No mass-generated mutex or prerequisite edits are permitted. Each country receives a report and a manual proposal before implementation.

## Denmark — pre-edit structural report

### Current true forks

- The political opening offers three government outcomes. The centre parliamentary route is explicitly mutex with the royal and emergency routes; all three also carry existing `allow_branch` safeguards.
- The civilian economy chooses between insuring the merchant fleet and investing in Copenhagen naval workshops, then reconverges at the Odense expansion.
- The army chooses between island garrisons and a Jutland mobile reserve, then reconverges at officer-academy motorisation.

### Current reconvergence points

- The three political outcomes reconverge through an OR prerequisite at `AOEIW9_DEN_politics_settlement`.
- The two civilian investment alternatives reconverge through OR at `AOEIW9_DEN_eco_civ_5`.
- Infrastructure, science, air, navy, and both diplomatic sub-branches contain several AND gates. These are programme-completion gates rather than alternate-path diamonds.

### Current cross-branch links

- The Copenhagen political settlement opens the foreign-policy tree. This is the one clear causal cross-branch link.
- The economic and military capstones aggregate their own specialised sub-branches. No additional cross-link is needed merely to satisfy a count: Denmark's compact state programme already asks the player to develop all three services and the full industrial base.

### Long unbranched chains

- `AOEIW9_DEN_eco_civ_5` through `AOEIW9_DEN_eco_civ_9` is a five-focus pure chain. Its railway/public-works stages present distinct investment priorities but currently provide no choice.
- The political columns are five nodes long, but they follow a genuine opening regime choice and deliver a government outcome; splitting each column again would dilute that decision.

### Undercooked or contradictory endpoints

- The industrial and military capstones have multi-branch requirements and use the shared consolidation effects. They are mechanically substantial.
- The diplomatic capstone is structurally contradictory: it requires both `AOEIW9_DEN_dip_a_5` and `AOEIW9_DEN_dip_b_5`. Those focuses grant annexation war goals against Sweden and Germany respectively, so the supposed Straits settlement is gated behind completing both wars of expansion.

### Approved structural changes

1. Turn the post-review civilian programme into a real investment choice: the Danish National Railways expansion and Copenhagen public works become mutex alternatives after `AOEIW9_DEN_eco_civ_6`; they reconverge through OR at the existing civilian-engine capstone. Rewards and IDs remain unchanged.
2. Turn the two diplomatic theatres into alternatives. The Swedish and German opening focuses become mutex, and the final Baltic-gates focus accepts either completed theatre through OR.
3. Within each selected diplomatic theatre, make the military mission and economic pressure approaches mutex alternatives. The existing staff-talk focus becomes their OR reconvergence. This changes sequencing only; each existing reward stays attached to its focus.
4. Do not add artificial economic-to-military dependencies. They would create long crossing lines between vertically separated sections and would make early industrial development depend on the later defence tree without a file-backed political reason.

### Post-edit acceptance checks

- Exactly one Swedish/German diplomatic theatre can be selected in a campaign.
- Each theatre offers one mission/economic-pressure choice and reconverges before escalation.
- `AOEIW9_DEN_dip_final` is reachable after either complete theatre.
- The civilian programme offers one railway/public-works choice and reconverges without orphaning `AOEIW9_DEN_eco_civ_9`.
- The graph has no missing prerequisite, mutex, or position references and no prerequisite cycles.

## Norway — pre-edit structural report

### Current true forks

- The opening political settlement has three regime routes protected by the same established completion and `allow_branch` safeguards used elsewhere in the regional trees.
- Merchant-shipping guarantees and coastal-arsenal contracts are a real economic choice and reconverge at the Trondheim workshops.
- The army chooses between static fjord districts and mobile winter detachments before returning to a common staff-college programme.

### Current reconvergence points

- The three regimes reconverge through OR at the Oslo settlement; the economic and army alternatives also reconverge through OR.
- Science, infrastructure, naval development, and the capstones use AND gates to require completion of complementary projects.
- The Atlantic diplomatic branch divides into a mission and economic leverage, but currently requires both. It is an AND work queue rather than a decision.

### Current cross-branch links

- The completed Oslo political settlement opens the Northern Maritime Doctrine.
- The industrial and defence programmes otherwise remain parallel. For Norway this is defensible: early hydroelectric and merchant-shipping investment should not be locked behind a later military node.

### Long unbranched chains

- The civilian programme from the Trondheim expansion to the Bergen shipping board is a five-focus pure chain.
- The Swedish diplomatic campaign is also five focuses in a straight line, although its mission and economic-pressure stages describe approaches that can sensibly compete.

### Undercooked or contradictory endpoints

- Industrial and winter-defence capstones combine all of their relevant sub-branches and use substantial consolidation rewards.
- `AOEIW9_NOR_dip_final` requires annexation war goals against both Sweden and Britain. That makes Norway's strategy capstone a reward for simultaneous Scandinavian and Atlantic expansion rather than the conclusion to one coherent strategy.

### Approved structural changes

1. Make the railway second shift and hydroelectric public-works programme alternative investments after renewal of the Norwegian State Railways; reconverge them at the Bergen shipping capstone.
2. Make the Swedish and Atlantic campaigns mutex alternatives after the Northern Maritime Doctrine, with OR reconvergence at Norway's strategy capstone.
3. In the Swedish campaign, branch the mission and economic-leverage approaches directly from the campaign opening, make them mutex, and reconverge at the existing staff talks.
4. In the Atlantic campaign, make the already parallel mission and economic-leverage approaches mutex and change their existing AND gate to OR.
5. Keep all focus rewards, IDs, and country targets attached to their current focuses.

### Post-edit acceptance checks

- One external theatre and one method within it can be selected.
- The final strategy is reachable after either complete external campaign.
- The Bergen shipping focus remains reachable after either civilian investment.
- No prerequisite cycle, missing reference, or orphaned node is introduced.
