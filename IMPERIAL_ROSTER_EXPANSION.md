# Imperial Roster Expansion

Stand: 15. September 2026. This pass implements the user's request to make every previously proposed imperial or regional actor playable while preserving the established alternate-history order.

## 1936 imperial subjects

| Overlord | Playable administrations |
|---|---|
| Japan | Insulindian Federation (`INS`), Philippine administration (`PHI`), Korean Protectorate (`KOR`) |
| Britain | Newfoundland (`NFL`), Burma (`BRM`), Nigeria (`NGA`), East African Federation (`KEN`), Ceylon (`SRL`) |
| France | Algeria (`ALG`), Morocco (`MOR`), Tunisia (`TUN`) |
| Belgium | Belgian Congo (`COG`) |
| Italy | Libya (`LBA`), Italian East Africa (`IAF`) |
| Spain | Cuba (`CUB`) |
| Persia | Afghanistan (`AFG`), South Azerbaijan (`AZR`) |
| Portugal | Angola (`ANG`), Mozambique (`MZB`) |
| Germany | Iceland (`ICE`); German East Asia remains `MAL` |

Every administration owns and cores a compact geographic block, receives a distinct starting national spirit and can use the Imperial Administration decisions. Local budget negotiations raise autonomy; accepting an imperial defence quota trades autonomy for war support and army experience; from 1939 a player can convene a constitutional conference. Existing `MAL`, `EST`, `VIN`, `MLI`, `KAZ` and `GEO` administrations also participate. German East Asia no longer recruits the inherited Malaysian/British-Malayan character roster.

## Chinese regional governments

The 1936 start now exposes five compact regional actors while preserving CHI's national cores:

- `SIK`: Taklamakan, Dihua, Dzungaria, Yarkand, Ruoqiang, Dabancheng and Kunlun Shan;
- `TIB`: Chang-Tang, Western Kham, Ganzi, Ue-Tsang and Ngari Khorsum;
- `YUN`: Yunnan, Dali and Liangshan;
- `XSM`: Gansu, Yushu, Songpan, Qinghai, Dulan, Jiuquan, Ejin, Pailingmiao, Wuwei and Guyuan;
- `XIC`: Xi'an, Yan'an and Yulin.

The Qing transfer effects no longer seize those states or add Qing cores there. The regional governments therefore remain playable parties during the opening crisis rather than disappearing during its first week. Each receives a unique political/economic constraint and two regional settlement decisions.

## Dynamic imperial formations

`MAF` (Mittelafrika) and `GPA` (German Pacific Administration) are new landless tags that Germany can establish after controlling their complete charter territories. Both receive their own identity modifier and become German subjects. `IAF` (Italian East Africa) is a new 1936 Italian colony holding Eritrea and Somalia. New tags have country definitions, history, localisation and complete flag sets at all three HOI4 sizes.

All three new administrations have dedicated fourteen-focus trees. Each tree develops its political settlement, economy, armed forces, external connection and a post-consolidation payoff. The two political and two military alternatives grant different permanent modifier categories: appointed imperial command competes with local consultation, while locally recruited forces compete with mobile or naval projection. Economic alternatives also choose between distinct infrastructure, industry or dockyard outcomes. The branches reconverge only after preserving those lasting differences. Titles and descriptions use the named ports, roads, institutions and forces of each administration rather than a shared generic template.

The other newly exposed tags retain their established RT56 country trees where available and gain the AOE scenario layer: correct compact territory, subject relationship, individual identity spirit, player introduction and recurring autonomy or regional-settlement decisions. This avoids replacing functioning country content with duplicate generic trees.

## Preserved boundaries

The Ottoman Empire remains contiguous across Anatolia, the Levant and Mesopotamia. No Syrian, Iraqi, Palestinian, Kurdish or Armenian start state was created. Spain retains imperial cores in Cuba, matching the established ARG/PRU convention; Persia retains its claims in Afghanistan and South Azerbaijan; CHI retains national cores across the regional governments. State and province geometry is unchanged.
