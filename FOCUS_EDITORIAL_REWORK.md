# Focus-tree editorial rework

This pass applies to every playable country. It changes visible titles and descriptions while preserving focus IDs, prerequisites, effects, layout and the established alternate-history setting. A country is only marked complete after its political branches have a written story, templated language has been reviewed in context, and English/German localisation touched by the pass remains paired.

## Global audit

The repository contains 4,107 AOE focus assignments across 56 country/shared selectors. A first lexical screen found 506 titles using the recurrent vocabulary `Commission`, `Directorate`, `Office`, `Board`, `Register`, `Timetable`, `Cadre`, `Quota`, `Ledger`, `Committee`, `Council`, `Compact`, `Charter`, `Programme`, `Framework` or `Settlement`. These are review candidates rather than automatic errors: a real institution or law may retain such a word when it is specific to the branch.

Status: Germany, the United States, Sweden, Finland, Brazil and Italy are complete. Every other tag remains queued; the visual redesign must wait until the country pass is complete.

## Sweden — branch stories

### Defence and the constitution

The 1936 defence vote forces the Riksdag, Crown, Social Democrats and officer corps to say what armed neutrality will cost. A royal path gathers conservatives at Drottningholm, the parliamentary path makes the Defence Commission publish its choices, and the command path dismisses debate in favour of orders from Stockholm. Each route must still create a government that can call reserves and place contracts. Its vocabulary comes from Swedish institutions and public arguments rather than an invented chain of directorates.

### Iron, power and the people's home

Rearmament competes directly with the Folkhemmet for money, labour and building capacity. LKAB ore, Bofors at Karlskoga, KTH workshops and northern hydroelectric works give the bargain real places and firms. The branch develops from the budget dispute through contracts, dams, laboratories and workers' housing. It ends with an industrial system described by the things Sweden actually joins together: iron, power and precision workshops.

### Armed neutrality

The General Staff must defend its plans before elected deputies and then prove them in Sweden's forests, coastal waters and northern distances. Skövde gives armoured training a physical centre while the Baltic and Finland force neutrality to acquire limits. Stockholm must decide what it will promise Helsinki before a crisis makes the answer for it. The end state is an armed neutrality whose diplomatic obligations match its military capacity.

## Finland — branch stories

### The republic after Mäntsälä

White veterans, the presidency, the Eduskunta, industrial owners and Greater Finland activists still argue over who saved the state in 1918 and who may command it now. The parliamentary path isolates coup politics through law, while the expansionist path turns Karelia from a memory into a programme. Tampere's mills make the contest material: their owners can bargain with parliament or lose the gates to armed guards. The branch ends only when Helsinki has chosen a source of authority for the next eastern emergency.

### Forest, rapids and workshops

Finland's export income begins in its forests and on the rail route to Kotka, while industrial independence depends on Imatra's turbines and the Tampere works. Civil development opens roads and power lines; mobilisation standardises timber, machinery and repairs. Research moves beside the production line at the State Aircraft Factory instead of remaining inside a nameless council. The payoff is a small economy organised around winter, distance and vulnerable imports.

### The eastern frontier

The Mannerheim Line, Jaeger tradition and winter detachments offer different answers to a larger neighbour. Defensive preparation has to connect fortifications with artillery, supply and the ability to move in snow. Nordic cooperation is valuable only if missions and staff talks produce commitments before the frontier breaks. Finland's foreign policy therefore remains a choice between help that may arrive and preparation it controls itself.

## Brazil — branch stories

### Coffee states, Crown and Estado Novo

Vargas, the imperial court, provincial governors, coffee oligarchs and the army contest the authority of Rio. Parliament can anchor the empire in the Constitution of 1934 and give Lusophone delegates a lawful chamber; the authoritarian route closes Congress and rules from the Catete Palace as the Estado Novo. São Cristóvão gives the restored Crown a visible home once that conflict is settled. The alternate monarchy remains intact, but its politics borrow real Brazilian actors, buildings and constitutional language.

### Coffee, steel and the interior

Coffee receipts at Santos finance the attempt to build an economy that exports more than raw goods. Volta Redonda provides the steel-making payoff, while Manaus and Amazon river depots turn the interior from a slogan into routes, workshops and medical surveys. State contracts connect coffee, rails, machine tools and armament orders. The result is a Brazilian market whose scale can be seen in ports, furnaces and navigable rivers.

### Army and South Atlantic

The army standardises weapons through the Realengo Arsenal while the navy and air arm face the distances of the South Atlantic. Rio and Lisbon negotiate investment and imperial defence as reciprocal obligations, not decorative friendship. Brazil can then convert those agreements into patrol zones, convoy routes and regional command. A South Atlantic victory therefore creates continuing responsibilities after the diplomatic claim.

## Italy — branch stories

### Crown, corporations and communes

The House of Savoy, Fascist party, industrial confederations and revolutionary councils all claim to embody the mass state. The corporate route must make the Carta del Lavoro function in wages and arbitration, while the communal route spreads authority beyond Turin through recallable deputies and disciplined militias. Caproni and La Spezia show how factory and sailors' councils acquire aircraft and ships. The existing Grand Council remains because it is a real institution; invented councils are replaced where they add no history or conflict.

### IRI and industrial mobilisation

IRI holds together banks and firms rescued by the state, but mobilisation still depends on FIAT, ILVA, Ansaldo, electric power and usable roads. The Autostrada dei Laghi becomes the starting point for a network tested by military traffic rather than a generic motorway programme. Marconi and the CNR connect prestigious science to radios, ships and factories. Workers' case popolari beside new routes expose the social cost beneath the regime's concrete spectacle.

### The services and Mediterranean ambition

The grandi manovre force Comando Supremo to confront paper divisions, weak signals and late supply before war does. The army, Regia Aeronautica and Regia Marina then build competing operational answers to Alpine and Mediterranean commitments. Palazzo Venezia turns corporate advice into personal orders when the dictatorship wins the constitutional struggle. Imperial ambition ends in the question of who rules from Rome and who carries the obligations created by its victories.

## United States — branch stories

### Constitutional breakdown

The Depression turns the dispute between the White House, Congress and state governors into a contest over who can still issue lawful orders. A workers' government can carry the red stars to Washington, while a military administration or a Pacific secession tests whether the Union survives by consent. The path ends only when taxes, contracts and mobilisation again answer to a constitution. Its language should sound like American headlines, speeches and constitutional arguments rather than another European ministry chart.

### Roads, laboratories and the arsenal

The Bureau of Public Roads, Detroit plants, Maxwell Field, NACA at Langley and the Signal Corps laboratories provide the real machinery of mobilisation. Civil works become defence roads; automobile dies become armament tools; air doctrine moves from classroom arguments to exercises and radar sets. The scientific escalation runs from Goddard's rockets through the Uranium Committee to the Chicago pile. The result is the Arsenal of Democracy, built through named places and agencies rather than generic boards.

### The hemisphere

The Good Neighbor policy has to become a reciprocal bargain rather than a slogan masking intervention. The Havana meeting gives collective defence a real diplomatic scene and forces the American republics to respond. A federal breakdown on the Pacific coast is written through Sacramento and Washington, while restored government brings governors back into the capital. The branch ends with a working continental order whose legitimacy depends on how those choices were made.

## Germany — branch stories

### The federal settlement

The Kaiser, Reichstag and larger kingdoms all claim the right to define the restored order. Bavaria, Saxony and Württemberg will support a federal monarchy only if Berlin accepts enforceable limits over budgets and mobilisation. Industry and organised labour then test whether constitutional government can make economic decisions without returning to decree. A revived Reichswirtschaftsrat and a second Locarno turn the domestic compromise into a recognisable policy abroad.

### The council revolution

Ruhr miners, railwaymen, the Roter Frontkämpferbund and factory delegates turn industrial unrest into a general strike. At a new Reichsrätekongress, national unions demand central quotas while works councils defend shop-floor sovereignty. Victory gives the councils ministries and factories but no functioning national supply system. The later focuses therefore move through seizure of power, the production argument, military reconstruction and an industrial endgame rather than a row of invented offices.

### Rearmament and the social bargain

The crown, Länder treasuries, General Staff, Ruhr employers and unions fight over who pays for preparedness. Stinnes–Legien offers a historical language for industrial peace; Kummersdorf, Döberitz, Krupp, Borsig and the Reichsbahn give procurement and training physical places. The conflict escalates through budgets, housing, rail capacity, foreign exchange and fuel stocks. Germany emerges ready only when its paper plans match actual workers, rolling stock and reserves.

### Parliamentary Germany

Opening the Reichstag begins a struggle among Social Democrats, liberals, Christian federalists, Communists and the Reichs-Landbund. Each faction speaks through its own constituency: municipal Siedlungen and social insurance for the SPD, Article 48 and police restraint for liberals, Länder rights for Christian democrats, bread and rent for the KPD, protected agriculture for conservatives. Coalition policy now reads like rival campaign programmes rather than interchangeable administrative settlements.

### Continental war and its aftermath

The authoritarian path inherits rival party, police and army administrations across occupied Europe. Berlin first imposes a single coercive command, then secures named resource routes and studies the failures recorded in campaign reports. The shared military branch brings air, armour, artillery and supply together in field language. Its payoff asks how the system survives its founders, giving the war machine a political consequence after victory.

## Why the German titles changed

The old names were changed where they described an invented container rather than an action, actor or conflict. `Ruhr Works Directorate`, `National Production Ledger`, `Military Freight Timetable` and similar titles could have belonged to almost any ideology; their replacements identify Essen, the Reichsbahn, named firms, campaign language or a factional demand. Repeated `Settlement`, `Commission`, `Board` and `Directorate` endings were replaced with slogans, questions, places, newspaper-like lines and historically grounded institutions. Existing concrete titles such as `The Reichstag Fire Decree`, `The Four Year Plan`, `The Colonial Office`, `Pax Germanica`, `Fortress Europe` and `The Imperial Defence Council` were deliberately retained.

Forty-three German focus title/description pairs were rewritten in English and German. No script identifier or gameplay effect changed.
